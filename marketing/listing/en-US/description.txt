Your coding agent needs an answer. Send it from your phone.
Herdwick connects to herdr on your own machines over SSH.
Read agent conversations, review tool steps and reply from iPhone or iPad.

ONE INBOX
See agents across your saved hosts. The ones that need you come first.

READ THE CONVERSATION
View transcripts from omp, Claude Code and Codex as chat. Expand tool steps, plans and diffs. Open the terminal for other agents or unavailable transcripts.

ANSWER IN PLACE
Choose an answer to a supported question or permission prompt, or write a reply. The terminal is always available.

USE THE TERMINAL
Live output, a message composer and keys for Esc, Tab, arrows, Enter and Ctrl-C. Optional typing mode takes control and resizes the host pane.

CONNECT YOUR MACHINES
Use SSH with a device-generated key or password. Tailscale sign-in and a machine picker are built in; Tailscale SSH requires host and tailnet configuration. No extra Herdwick service to install.

KEEP YOUR PLACE
The last session snapshot stays visible while Herdwick reconnects. Choose from seven terminal themes, with separate light and dark settings.

TRY THE DEMO
Explore a scripted host before connecting your own. No Herdwick account required.

YOUR CONNECTION
The SSH private key stays in the device Keychain. Messages and attachments go to your host, not a Herdwick server. Optional Tailscale use is subject to its privacy policy.

REQUIREMENTS
Requires iOS 26 or later and a reachable Mac or Linux host running herdr. Coding agents run on your host, not on your phone.
