Answer your coding agents from your phone.
Herdwick connects to herdr on your own Mac or Linux machines over SSH. See what your agents did and reply from iPhone or iPad.

ONE INBOX
Every agent on your saved hosts, in one list. The ones waiting on you come first.

READ THE CONVERSATION
Conversations from omp, Claude Code and Codex show as a chat. Tool steps fold into one line; open it for detail. Edits show as diffs, line by line. Other agents, and transcripts that aren't available, open in the terminal. A line marks what's new since you last looked.

ANSWER
Tap an option on a supported question or permission prompt, or type a reply. The terminal is always there.

USE THE TERMINAL
Live output, a message box and keys for Esc, Tab, arrows, Enter and Ctrl-C. Typing mode is optional; it takes control and resizes the pane on your host.

CONNECT YOUR MACHINES
Use SSH with a key made on your device, or a password. Tailscale sign-in and a machine picker are built in; Tailscale SSH needs setup on your host and tailnet. Nothing to install for Herdwick on your host beyond herdr.

KEEP YOUR PLACE
While Herdwick reconnects, your last view stays on screen. Pick from seven terminal themes, set separately for light and dark.

TRY THE DEMO
Look around a scripted demo host before you connect your own. No Herdwick account needed.

PRIVACY
Your SSH private key stays in the device Keychain. Messages and attachments go to your host, not to a Herdwick server. No analytics. If you use Tailscale, its privacy policy applies.

REQUIREMENTS
iOS 26 or later, and a Mac or Linux host running herdr that you can reach. Your agents run on the host, not on your phone.
