Herdwick 1.0

• Every coding agent on your machines in one list, sorted by who needs you first.
• The live terminal of any pane, sized for your screen. Reply from the composer or take over typing.
• Tailscale built in: sign in, pick a machine, connect with Tailscale SSH. No keys to copy.
• Or plain SSH, with a key made on this device or a password.
• Keeps the last snapshot on screen through a dropped connection and picks up in place.
• Seven terminal themes, light and dark.
• A built-in demo host, so you can look around before connecting anything.
